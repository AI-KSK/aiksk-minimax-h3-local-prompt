# Camera & Action Recipes

## Stable dialogue shot

- medium or medium close-up;
- static / small slow push-in;
- reaction action only;
- no unnecessary orbit.

## Product hero

- locked hero composition;
- small controlled orbit or push-in;
- specular/material highlights move, geometry does not.

## Fight beat

- establish both bodies;
- define attack limb/weapon and direction;
- define contact/block;
- define reaction/recovery;
- one camera move.

## Transformation

- describe ordered changes;
- keep camera readable;
- end on a stable final form.

## FL2VA path

- first-frame state;
- continuous action changes;
- narrowing difference to last frame;
- exact final landing.
